**Pause from Crowbar's Guide menu** (optional, `"pause": true`). When the player presses Guide during a game, Crowbar asks the runtime to pause. The game stops (loop, sound, clocks) or refuses when it cannot stop now, and the Guide stays closed, as on a console. See [SPEC.md §4](https://github.com/DiamondDogz/crowbar-sdk/blob/main/SPEC.md#pause-from-the-guide-optional-pause-true).

- C: `crowbar_poll()` returns `CROWBAR_PAUSE`, `CROWBAR_RESUME` or `CROWBAR_NONE`; `crowbar_answer(1 or 0)` replies. The first poll writes `0 ready`.
- Python: `crowbar.poll()` and `crowbar.answer(True or False)`.
- `PS4RT_CONTROL_DIR` is passed to every runtime, whatever its launch format.
- The controller belongs to Crowbar while paused; buttons still held after resume are ignored until released.
- Schema and `crowbar-check` know `features.pause`.

Also in the SPEC: what Crowbar shares with the player's friends about trophies (names and grades of new unlocks, numbers and dates for comparison). Runtimes have nothing to do.
